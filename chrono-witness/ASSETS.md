# Asset credits and attribution

Every runtime asset is in `public/assets/` and is produced by the scripts in `tools/assets/`. This list
names every third-party input, what was taken from it and its licence. Inputs from the game itself are
listed so it is clear that nothing else was used.

## Third-party inputs

### MakeHuman: base mesh, morph targets, skeleton, skin weights, eyes (CC0 1.0)
- **Source:** <https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman>, folder `makehuman/data/`, commit `a8bc2d5`.
- **Files used:**
  - `3dobjs/base.obj` (hm08 base mesh)
  - `targets/macrodetails/**`, plus face targets in `targets/{head,nose,chin,mouth,ears,eyebrows,eyes,cheek,forehead,neck}`
  - `rigs/default.mhskel` and `rigs/default_weights.mhw`, reduced to 63 bones
  - `eyes/high-poly/high-poly.{obj,mhclo}`, with the texture replaced by our own
- **Licence:** CC0 1.0 Universal ("This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020";
  `LICENSE.ASSETS.md` in that repository).
- **Copyright holders named in the asset headers at release:**
  - Data Collection AB (https://www.datacollection.se), Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier
  - targets: "Original copyright (C) 2014 Manuel Bastioni, transferred to the above in 2016"
- **Derived:**
  - the body, head and hand geometry of every character and of the first-person arms
  - body shape variation
  - the joint positions of the shared skeleton
  - the skin weights, which were also transferred to the garments

## Original work for this project (no third-party content)

- **Code:** all game code, procedural geometry (terrain, trenches, dugouts, ruins, props; London terraces,
  streets, shops, the Underground station, vehicles, dockland silhouettes, searchlights and barrage
  balloons) and shaders.
- **Textures:** all environment PBR texture sets and effect textures in `public/assets/textures/`,
  procedurally generated by `tools/assets/textures/` (numpy, scipy, OpenCV). See its README. This includes
  the London sets (stock brick, slate, flags, tarmac, stucco, wallpaper, floorboards, station tiles,
  platform concrete, sandbags, soot) and the fire flipbook, dark smoke, window tape and glass decals.
- **Characters:**
  - clothing, webbing, helmets, hair, all character textures and the first-person arms, generated by
    `tools/assets/characters/`
  - the hand-keyed clips: `lie_supine`, `kneel_work`, `crouch_cover`, the pointing arm, stretcher arms,
    breathing and all `fp_*` clips
- **Audio:** all procedural WebAudio. No audio files.
- **Text:** all dialogue, mission text, in-world signs (including the London street, station and ARP
  signs, drawn at runtime), the message form contents and the diary text.
  These are fiction and are labelled as such in game.
- **Historical research notes:** `docs/history/`. They cite their sources; quotations are short and attributed.

Fonts: the UI uses the player's system fonts. No font files are shipped.
