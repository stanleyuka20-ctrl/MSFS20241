## Characters (`public/assets/characters/`)

All character meshes, garments, gear, helmet, textures, the first-person arms and the
hand-keyed / procedural animation layers are **own work** generated by the scripts in
`tools/assets/characters/` (procedural geometry + procedural textures painted in 3D with
numpy; no third-party images). They are derived from the following third-party inputs:

### MakeHuman base mesh, morph targets, skeleton, skin weights and eyes — CC0 1.0
- Source: MakeHuman 1.x asset repository, <https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman>
  (folder `makehuman/data/`; commit used: `a8bc2d5`), files used:
  `3dobjs/base.obj` (hm08 base mesh), `targets/macrodetails/**` (gender/age/muscle/weight/height/
  proportion targets), `targets/{head,nose,chin,mouth,ears,eyebrows,eyes,cheek,forehead,neck}/*.target`
  (face shape targets), `rigs/default.mhskel` + `rigs/default_weights.mhw` (skeleton joints and skin
  weights; reduced to 63 bones, dropped bones' weights merged into kept bones),
  `eyes/high-poly/high-poly.{obj,mhclo}` (eyeball mesh; texture replaced by our own), `uvs` of the base mesh.
- Licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal** ("This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020",
  see `LICENSE.ASSETS.md` in that repository).
- Copyright holders named in the asset headers at the time of the CC0 release:
  Copyright (C) 2020 Data Collection AB (https://www.datacollection.se), Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier;
  targets: "Original copyright (C) 2014 Manuel Bastioni, transferred to the above in 2016";
  rig/weights: "(c) 2020/2021 Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier".
- What was derived: the body/head/hand geometry of all characters and of `fp_arms.glb`, body shape
  variation, joint positions of the shared skeleton, skin weights (also transferred to the garments).

### CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (BVH conversion by B. Hahne / cgspeed)
- Original database: <http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/> — Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab.
  "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with
  funding from NSF EIA-0196217."
- BVH conversion: "MotionBuilder-friendly BVH conversion release of the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture
  Database" by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com), 2010 release, obtained from the public GitHub mirror
  <https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh> (commit `3bc9c75`), files
  `Sequence-*/NN/Data/NN_MM.zip`.
- Licence / terms (from the CMU site and the conversion's READMEFIRST): "Use this data! This data is
  free for use in research and commercial projects worldwide." CMU: "You may include this data in
  commercially-sold products, but you may not resell this data directly, even in converted form."
  Bruce Hahne: "CMU places no restrictions on the use of the original dataset, and I (Bruce) place no
  additional restrictions on the use of this particular BVH conversion."
- Takes used (retargeted onto our skeleton, cut into loops, made in-place, foot-locked):
  `08_01` (walk), `09_11` (run), `35_01` (walk; base of carry_walk and carry_box), `91_16` (limp),
  `77_02` (standing; idle and base of point, wave and talk_worried), `111_28` (standing still, hands
  clasped; idle_alt), `19_08` (conversation with hand gestures; talk), `82_05` (sitting on ground
  relaxing; sit_ground).
  `lie_supine`, `kneel_work`, `crouch_cover`, `lie_sleep`, `sit_huddle`, `dig`, `kneel_listen`, the
  pointing / waving / hand-wringing arms, the stretcher-handle and box-carrying arms, breathing, the
  procedural jaw and all `fp_*` clips are hand-keyed/procedural (own work).

### London 1940 civilian cast
`warden_woman`, `mother_1940`, `girl_1940`, `boy_1940`, `old_man_1940`, `rescue_man_a`, `rescue_man_b`,
`nurse_1940`, `wvs_woman`, `afs_fireman`, `civilian_man`, `civilian_man_b`, `civilian_woman`,
`civilian_woman_b`, `civilian_oldwoman`, `civilian_oldwoman_b`: same sources as above (MakeHuman CC0
body, targets, rig and eyes; CMU mocap via the cgspeed BVH conversion for the clips listed).
Clothing, headwear, gas-mask haversack/carton, helmet lettering, armband lettering, blanket/scarf
patterns and all textures are own procedural work; no third-party images, fonts or logos.
